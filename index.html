Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from collectors, buyers and artists.

The story of Arttent is one of continuous evolution and a profound commitment to the advancement of contemporary art in Georgia and beyond. After a period of strategic reflection, Arttent is relaunching with a focused vision: to serve as the essential catalyst and connective tissue for the regional art ecosystem. Founded in July 2019, Arttent initially disrupted the traditional gallery model with its innovative "Pop Up Gallery Concept." This pioneering approach, which activated unexpected spaces like ARTTENT MTATSMINDA and the highly visible ARTTENT TBILISI MALL, successfully made art accessible to a wider public and established a new standard for engagement. This foundational experience—connecting artists with new audiences in dynamic, high-traffic environments—is the bedrock of our expertise. Today, Arttent transcends its original gallery function to become a dedicated Art Initiatives and Development Organization. We are building upon our legacy of making art accessible by focusing on strategic partnerships, artist advocacy, and opportunity creation.
